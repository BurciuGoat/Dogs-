The Saviors of Modesto

Before they were the faces of this premium boutique, Daisy (the brilliant brown Yorkie Poodle Shih Tzu mix) and Biscuit (the fearless white Yorkie Poodle) were busy saving the world. When global nuclear threats descended upon the city of Modesto, it wasn't the military that stepped up—it was these two.

Disarming warheads with precise paw-swipes and deflecting lasers with sheer fluff, they single-handedly thwarted armageddon. Today, these revered worldwide heroes wear impeccably tailored suits, negotiate treaties with international diplomats, and advise the President on matters of global security.

Having secured world peace, Daisy and Biscuit have dedicated their post-heroic lives to a new, equally noble cause: providing essential, high-quality goods for common civilians. Welcome to their exclusive collection.
